Privacy policy

Last updated November 20, 2026.

Kid Quest TV is a learning adventure app for Apple TV, made for children about 4 to 8 and the grown-ups who watch with them. This policy describes how the app and this site are designed to work.

We do not collect personal information

Kid Quest TV does not ask for a name, email address, birthday, or any other personal information in the app. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

No ads, no tracking, no analytics

The app has no advertisements. It does not include tracking software, advertising identifiers, or analytics. It does not use third-party software development kits. It does not sell or share information for advertising or marketing.

No network features

The app is designed to work on the Apple TV without talking to our servers. It does not require an internet connection to play.

Voice stays on the device

Kids can steer on-screen choices with their voice when that feature is available. When it is used, speech is recognized on the Apple TV itself. Audio is not sent off the device for recognition, and we do not receive or store voice recordings.

If on-device recognition is not available, or if permission to use the microphone is not given, the voice feature is hidden. The Siri Remote remains a full way to play.

This website

This site is static. It does not use analytics, advertising, or other tracking scripts. It does not set cookies.

Children

Kid Quest TV is meant for a young audience. Because the app does not collect personal information, create accounts, show ads, or send voice off the device, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy are welcome at support@kidquesttv.com.
