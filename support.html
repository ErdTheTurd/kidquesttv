Support

For help with Kid Quest TV, email support@kidquesttv.com.

When will the app be available?

Kid Quest TV is coming to Apple TV. It is not in the App Store yet. This website is an outline of the app, not a store listing.

How do you play?

On the Apple TV, use the Siri Remote to choose one of the two large options on each screen. If voice is available, a child may also say the choice they see — for example “Start,” “3,” or “Tadpole.”

What if voice does not work?

The Siri Remote is a full way to play. Voice is hidden if on-device recognition is not available, or if permission to use the microphone is not given. Recognition, when used, stays on the Apple TV and is not sent off the device.

Do we need an account?

No. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, and no tracking. The app does not talk to our servers.

Who is it for?

The app is made for children about 4 to 8, and for families who want a short learning adventure on the TV.

What’s in the first quest?

Lumen crosses a lily-pond path. Kids pick a way across, count three frogs on the pads, answer “A frog starts life as a…?” (Tadpole), and make one kind choice. The quest ends on a celebration screen.
