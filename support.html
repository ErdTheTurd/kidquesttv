Support

For help with Kid Quest TV, email support@kidquesttv.com.

When will the app be available?

Kid Quest TV is coming soon to Apple TV. It is not in the App Store yet. This website is an outline of the app.

How do you play?

On the Apple TV, use the Siri Remote to choose one of the two large options on each screen. If voice is available, a child may also say the choice they see.

What if voice does not work?

The Siri Remote is a full way to play. Voice is hidden if on-device recognition is not available, or if permission to use the microphone or speech recognition is not given. Recognition, when used, stays on the Apple TV and is not sent off the device.

Do we need an account?

No. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, and no tracking.

Who is it for?

The app is made for children about 4 to 8, and for families who want a short educational adventure on the TV.
