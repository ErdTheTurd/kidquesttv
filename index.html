What’s in the first quest

The outline app, as it stands. Nothing more.

  • Lily-Pond Path

    Lumen needs a way across. Kids choose Stepping Stones or Wooden Bridge, then a kind thing to do: Leave a Lantern or Share a Berry.

  • Counting Frogs

    Three frogs sit on the pads. The question is “How many frogs are on the pads?” with the choices 3 and 5. A miss gets a calm hint: “Almost — take another look and try again.”

  • One frog fact

    The question is “A frog starts life as a…?” The choices are Tadpole and Caterpillar. After a correct answer, the app states the fact: “A frog starts life as a tadpole.”

  • Steer with your voice

    When on-device speech is available, kids can say the choice they see — “Start,” “Parents,” “3,” “Tadpole,” and the rest. If it isn’t available, voice is hidden and the Siri Remote is a full way to play.

  • A page for parents

    The Parents screen says there are no ads, no tracking, no accounts, and no network features. When voice is used, recognition stays on that Apple TV.
