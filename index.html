What it is

A short quest. A few choices. Something to notice along the way.

Kid Quest TV is an educational adventure made for the living-room screen. In the first quest, Lumen walks a lily-pond path. Children pick a way across, count the frogs on the pads, and meet one simple, accurate animal fact: a frog starts life as a tadpole.

The story is short on purpose. Big type, two clear choices at a time, and a calm ending. It is meant to be played together, or by a child with a remote in hand.

Steer with your voice

When voice is available, kids can say the choice on the screen. If it isn’t, the Siri Remote is a full way to play.

Learn as you go

The first quest includes a simple counting question and one animal fact. We only use facts we are sure are accurate.

Made for Apple TV

Large, focusable choices designed for the Siri Remote and the living-room couch. No account to set up.

Privacy

No ads. No tracking. No accounts. Voice stays on the device.

  • ✓There are no ads, and nothing is sold or shared for advertising.
